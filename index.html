Our Story

«Grim & Grim» was born from a desire to create music that is felt with the heart. We began our journey in a small studio in the center of Kyiv, where every chord, every word was filled with emotion and passion. It was a place where our first songs were born, where we learned to play together as a single organism.

Over the years of our activity, we have performed on dozens of stages, from small clubs to large festivals. Our music is a blend of traditional rock with modern elements, making it unique and instantly recognizable. We are not afraid to experiment with sound, adding electronic elements or folk instruments.

Every one of our shows is not just a concert, but a true event where we share our energy with the audience and create an unforgettable atmosphere. We believe that music has the power to unite people, build communities, and inspire change.

Our band consists of seasoned musicians, each bringing their own unique style and vision. Maksym crafts incredible guitar riffs, Olena enchants with her vocals, and Taras holds the rhythm that makes hearts beat in unison with the music.
